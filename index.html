About Us

AGUEDA Del Giudice

FOUNDER & PRINCIPAL

At Funzione Design Studio, we believe that design doesn't begin with a style, but with the lives of the people who inhabit the space.

We are an architecture and interior design studio dedicated to creating deeply personalized spaces. Every decision stems from a true understanding of our clients: their lifestyle, routines, family dynamics, surroundings, and everything else that shapes their daily lives.

Discover More →

Our process begins with a thorough exploration—not just of the space itself, but of the people within it. We analyze the context, the climate, and the existing architecture, seamlessly blending this information with the tastes, moods, materials, colors, and inspirations that resonate with each individual client.

We firmly believe that a space can be visually stunning, but if it fails to meet the real needs of the people living in it, it will eventually lose its value over time.

That is why, in every project, FUNZIONE is the guiding principle behind everything we do. We design spaces that don't just look good—they work, they feel right, and they endure. These are spaces meant to be lived in, tailored to each unique lifestyle, and developed through a highly personal, meticulous, and mindful process.

At Funzione Design Studio, we don't design simply to impress.

We design to transform the way you experience your space.
